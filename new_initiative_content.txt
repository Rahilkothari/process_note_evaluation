- Legal/operational readiness for partnerships.	MoU draft, Letter of Understanding, or a concept note with partner org’s scope of work, deliverables, and timelines.

4	Letters of Intent / Government Endorsements	-Confirms that government or stakeholders are on board.
- Builds project credibility 	Signed letters, emails, or notifications from departments (Education, Skill, Women & Child, Rural Dev., etc.) expressing approval/support
5	Baseline Data or Need Assessment	- Proves that the project is needed in this area.
- Helps track change in the future.	Primary survey reports, UDISE data, ASER/FLN results, community needs assessments
6	Risk Matrix	- Identifies key operational, financial, reputational, or legal risks.
- Lists mitigation strategies.	Excel/Word table listing risks, likelihood, impact, mitigation plan, responsible team. Can follow a 5x5 risk assessment grid

7	Resource Materials / Prototype Samples (if applicable)	- Shows that tools and materials are ready or being developed.
- Useful for training or field use.	Draft material, training module, teacher handbook, app screenshots, sample question papers, mobile lab photos, etc.

8	Theory of Change / Logical Framework (Log frame)	- Explains how the project will lead to long-term impact.
- Connects inputs to outcomes.	Diagram or table with Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Outcomes → Impact.
 Mention any assumptions or external factors.

9	Monitoring & Evaluation Plan	- Framework to measure progress and outcomes.
- Clarifies frequency, tools, roles, and budget for M&E
	M&E Matrix or Dashboard template, toolkits, MIS structure, frequency of data collection, reporting schedule.
10	Stakeholder Mapping Matrix	- Lists key people/organizations involved.
- Helps build better coordination and support.	Table/chart with stakeholders, interest level, influence, proposed engagement method, frequency.
11	Communication & Advocacy Plan	 - Tells how the project will share its work with others.
- Helps build visibility and partnerships.	Simple plan listing: audience groups, communication channels (social media, events, reports), key messages, timeline 
12	Case Studies / Success Stories (if scaling/continuing existing work)	- Shows proof that similar work has worked before.
- Builds confidence in the proposal.	1–2 page write-ups with photos, quotes from beneficiaries, impact data, and KEF attribution.
